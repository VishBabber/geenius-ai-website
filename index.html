The leak

The calls you never hear about

A missed call does not appear on your accounts as a line marked lost revenue. The phone rings while you are with a customer, or up a ladder, or at seven in the evening when the office is shut, and the person on the other end just tries the next name on the list. You never find out what they wanted, what the job was worth, or that they rang at all. Most owners guess it is a handful a week. When we actually count them, it rarely is.

Voicemail does not fix it, because almost nobody leaves one any more. An answering service takes a name and a number but cannot tell a serious enquiry from a supplier calling about an invoice. Both of them still leave you ringing people back at nine the next morning, by which point somebody else has already quoted.

Guardrails

Built so it does not embarrass you

Every one of these exists because an owner asked us what happens when it goes wrong.

It hears when someone is annoyed

It reads tone and intent, not just words. If a caller gets frustrated or asks something it should not be answering, it hands them to a human rather than talking in circles.

One agent, every inbox

The same agent answers your phone, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs and SMS. Customers get the same answer wherever they ask.

It will not make things up

Guardrails keep it to what it genuinely knows about your business. If the answer is not in your knowledge base it says so and takes a message, rather than guessing at your prices.

You see every conversation

A transcript lands in your email or by text as soon as the call ends. You can read exactly what was said, and exactly what you would have missed.

Anything valuable comes to you

You decide what it handles and what gets passed on. Big enquiries, complaints and anything unusual go to your team, or to ours if you would rather.

It sounds like your business

Pick the accent, the tone and the greeting. You can clone your own voice if you want callers to hear you answer.
