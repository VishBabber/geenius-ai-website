The calls younever hear about

A missed call does not appear on your accounts as a line marked lost revenue. The phone rings while you are with a customer, or up a ladder, or at seven in the evening when the office is shut, and the person on the other end just tries the next name on the list. You never find out what they wanted, what the job was worth, or that they rang at all. Most owners guess it is a handful a week. When we actually count them, it rarely is.

Voicemail does not fix it, because almost nobody leaves one any more. An answering service takes a name and a number but cannot tell a serious enquiry from a supplier calling about an invoice. Both of them still leave you ringing people back at nine the next morning, by which point somebody else has already quoted.
